Frequently Asked Questions

Can I change my plan later?

▼

Yes, you can upgrade or downgrade your plan at any time. When upgrading, you'll be billed the prorated difference immediately. When downgrading, the new rate will take effect at the start of your next billing cycle.

What payment methods do you accept?

▼

We accept all major credit cards, including Visa, MasterCard, American Express, and Discover. We also support payments via PayPal. For Enterprise plans, we can arrange for bank transfers or invoicing.

Is there a free trial?

▼

Yes, we offer a 14-day free trial on all our plans. No credit card is required to start your trial. At the end of your trial, you can choose the plan that best fits your needs.

What happens if I exceed my plan limits?

▼

If you exceed your plan's limits, we won't cut you off immediately. We'll notify you and give you the option to upgrade to a higher plan. If you continue to exceed your limits without upgrading, we may limit some features until you upgrade or reduce your usage.
